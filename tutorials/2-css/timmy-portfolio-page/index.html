Hello, I'm Timmy, My overall goal in creating this portfolio and presenting my work to my peers is to be able to express my creativity through various forms of digital media. I created this site to showcase my artwork and projects, and I want people to be able to see the different forms of media that I explore. By sharing my work with others, people will be able to see the different types of projects that I enjoy creating, as well as the creativity behind them. Ultimately, I believe that this portfolio will represent not just my passion for creating but also exploring different media to express that creativity.
